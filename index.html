About Me

Hello, I’m Pedro.

I’m Pedro, a data enthusiast from Brazil.

I am currently pursuing a B.A. in computer science at Denison University, where I hope to focus on the intersection of music and data. I am interested in how categories such as genre shape and inform the way we understand music and artists.

I am currently working on tracing recurring lyrical themes across ambient albums from the last decade. My latest data project assesses the execution of another project concerned with the underrepresentation of women in songwriting credits.

My past experience includes a data engineering role at the startup Bluely and a translation research position at tourism magazine EMBRATUR (PT-EN).
